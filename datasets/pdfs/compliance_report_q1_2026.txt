EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This report presents the findings of the Q1 2026 internal compliance review conducted across all departments of Nextera Technologies Inc. The review covered financial controls, regulatory adherence, data handling practices, and vendor management compliance. A total of 47 compliance checks were performed, with 39 passing satisfactorily and 8 requiring corrective action. The overall compliance score for Q1 2026 is 82.9%, a decrease from 89.4% in Q4 2025. Three critical findings require immediate executive attention.

AUDIT FINDINGS

1. FINANCIAL CONTROLS (Score: 87%)

Audit of expense approvals revealed that 14 out of 312 reimbursement claims totaling $23,847 were processed without the required second-level manager approval. This was concentrated in the Sales department (9 claims) and Operations (5 claims). The root cause was traced to a misconfigured approval workflow in SAP Concur following the January 2026 system update. Additionally, three vendor payments totaling $156,200 to CloudStack Solutions were disbursed 15 days before the contracted delivery milestones, violating Section 4.2 of the Procurement Policy.

2. REGULATORY ADHERENCE (Score: 79%)

The company failed to submit the SOC 2 Type II attestation renewal documentation to two enterprise clients (Meridian Healthcare and Apex Financial Group) by the contractual deadline of February 28, 2026. The submissions were completed on March 12, 2026, resulting in a 12-day delay. Meridian Healthcare has issued a formal notice of non-compliance. Furthermore, 7 employees in the Engineering department had not completed the mandatory annual GDPR awareness training by the March 15, 2026 deadline, placing the company at risk of non-compliance with Article 39 of the GDPR.

3. DATA HANDLING PRACTICES (Score: 78%)

A review of data access logs identified that 3 employees in the IT department retained access to the production customer database after transferring to non-technical roles. Access was not revoked within the required 48-hour window per IT-POL-2026-007 Section 4. Additionally, 2 instances of Confidential-tier data were found stored on personal OneDrive accounts belonging to employees in the Finance department, in violation of the Data Classification Policy.

4. VENDOR MANAGEMENT (Score: 88%)

Two of the company's 23 active vendors (TechServ Global and DataBridge Analytics) had expired compliance certifications. TechServ Global's ISO 27001 certification expired on January 31, 2026 and was not renewed until March 22, 2026. DataBridge Analytics failed to provide updated SOC 2 documentation despite three formal requests initiated on February 5, 2026.

REMEDIATION STEPS

1. SAP Concur approval workflow has been corrected as of February 18, 2026 by the IT team. All affected expense claims have been retroactively approved by authorized managers.
2. A mandatory GDPR training session has been scheduled for April 22, 2026 for all non-compliant employees. Failure to attend will result in system access suspension.
3. IT Security has revoked the 3 stale database access accounts and implemented an automated role-change access review script effective March 25, 2026.
4. Finance department employees involved in the personal storage violation have completed remedial data handling training. Files have been migrated to approved SharePoint locations.
5. Procurement has issued final compliance certification requests to TechServ Global and DataBridge Analytics with a 30-day deadline. Failure to comply will trigger vendor contract review.
6. Legal is preparing a formal response to Meridian Healthcare's non-compliance notice, expected by April 30, 2026.

RECOMMENDATIONS

The Compliance team recommends quarterly access review audits, mandatory automated workflow testing after system updates, and the implementation of a vendor compliance tracking dashboard in ServiceNow. A follow-up review is scheduled for July 2026.
