1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes the information security standards and acceptable use guidelines for all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors accessing Nextera Technologies systems and data. Compliance is mandatory and violations may result in disciplinary action, termination, or legal proceedings.

2. PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS

All user accounts must adhere to the following password standards:
- Minimum length: 14 characters
- Must include at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one numeral, and one special character (!@#$%^&*)
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days
- The last 12 passwords cannot be reused
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is required for all accounts accessing internal systems, including email, VPN, and cloud platforms
- Passwords must not contain the user's name, employee ID, or common dictionary words
- Account lockout occurs after 5 consecutive failed login attempts, requiring IT Service Desk intervention (ext. 4500) to unlock

Employees must never share credentials with any person, including IT staff. IT personnel will never ask for your password.

3. VPN USAGE

Remote access to Nextera Technologies' internal network must be conducted exclusively through the approved corporate VPN (GlobalProtect v6.2 or later). Requirements include:
- VPN must be active before accessing any internal application, file share, or database
- Split tunneling is strictly prohibited
- VPN sessions will automatically disconnect after 30 minutes of inactivity
- Employees must connect only from secured, private networks; public Wi-Fi usage requires prior IT Security approval and an additional encrypted tunnel
- All VPN connections are logged and monitored by the Security Operations Center (SOC)
- Personal devices must be enrolled in the Mobile Device Management (MDM) system before VPN access is provisioned

4. DATA CLASSIFICATION

All company data must be classified according to the following tiers:
- PUBLIC: Marketing materials, published financial reports, press releases. No restrictions on distribution.
- INTERNAL: Internal memos, project plans, meeting notes. Restricted to Nextera employees only.
- CONFIDENTIAL: Employee records, customer PII, source code, financial projections. Restricted to authorized personnel with a documented business need. Must be encrypted at rest and in transit.
- RESTRICTED: Board communications, M&A documents, security audit findings, executive compensation data. Accessible only to named individuals with explicit CISO or CEO approval. Requires AES-256 encryption and access is logged with tamper-proof audit trails.

Employees are responsible for correctly classifying data they create or handle. Misclassification incidents must be reported within 24 hours to security@nextera-tech.com.

5. INCIDENT REPORTING

All employees are required to report suspected security incidents immediately. A security incident includes but is not limited to:
- Unauthorized access to systems or data
- Lost or stolen devices (laptops, phones, USB drives)
- Phishing emails or social engineering attempts
- Malware detection or unusual system behavior
- Accidental disclosure of Confidential or Restricted data

Reports must be submitted through the Incident Response Portal (incidents.nextera-tech.com) or by calling the 24/7 Security Hotline at +1-512-555-0199. Employees must preserve all evidence and refrain from attempting to investigate or remediate incidents independently. The SOC team will acknowledge all reports within 30 minutes during business hours and 2 hours outside business hours.

Failure to report a known security incident is a violation of this policy and may result in disciplinary action.
