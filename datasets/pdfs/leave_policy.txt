1. PURPOSE

This policy outlines the leave entitlements available to all full-time and part-time employees of Nextera Technologies Inc. It is designed to support employee well-being while maintaining operational efficiency across all departments.

2. ANNUAL LEAVE (PAID TIME OFF)

All full-time employees are entitled to paid annual leave based on tenure:
- 0 to 2 years of service: 15 business days per calendar year
- 3 to 5 years of service: 20 business days per calendar year
- 6 to 10 years of service: 25 business days per calendar year
- 10+ years of service: 30 business days per calendar year

Part-time employees accrue annual leave on a pro-rata basis. Unused annual leave may be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days into the following calendar year. Any excess days will be forfeited unless prior written approval is obtained from the department head. Leave requests must be submitted through the HR Portal (hrportal.nextera-tech.com) at least 10 business days in advance for periods exceeding 3 consecutive days.

3. SICK LEAVE

Employees are entitled to 12 paid sick days per calendar year. Sick leave may be used for personal illness, medical appointments, or care of an immediate family member. A medical certificate from a licensed physician is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive business days. Unused sick leave does not carry over and is not compensable upon separation from the company. Employees who exhaust their sick leave allocation may apply for extended medical leave through HR, subject to manager approval and supporting medical documentation.

4. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE

Nextera Technologies provides the following parental leave benefits:
- Maternity Leave: 16 weeks of fully paid leave for birth mothers, commencing up to 4 weeks before the expected delivery date. An additional 8 weeks of unpaid leave may be requested.
- Paternity Leave: 6 weeks of fully paid leave for fathers or non-birth parents, to be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption date.
- Adoption Leave: 12 weeks of fully paid leave for the primary caregiver, and 6 weeks for the secondary caregiver.

Employees must notify HR and their direct manager at least 60 days prior to the anticipated start of parental leave. All parental leave benefits apply to employees who have completed at least 12 months of continuous service.

5. BEREAVEMENT LEAVE

Employees are granted paid bereavement leave as follows:
- Immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling): 5 business days
- Extended family (grandparent, grandchild, in-law, aunt, uncle): 3 business days
- Close friend or colleague: 1 business day (manager discretion)

Additional unpaid bereavement leave of up to 5 days may be granted upon request. Employees must notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible and may be asked to provide documentation such as an obituary or death certificate for leave exceeding 3 days.

6. GENERAL PROVISIONS

All leave requests are subject to operational requirements and manager approval. Misuse of leave entitlements may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Employees on probation (first 90 days) are eligible for sick leave and bereavement leave only. Questions regarding this policy should be directed to hr-support@nextera-tech.com or ext. 4200.
